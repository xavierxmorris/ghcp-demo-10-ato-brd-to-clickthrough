1. Purpose

Micro and small businesses that lodge their own quarterly Business Activity Statement (BAS) do so today on a paper form or through a portal designed for a desktop. In the last four quarters, 21% of self-prepared statements needed a correction, and the single largest cause was an arithmetic error in the net amount at labels 7A / 7B — a figure the lodger cannot see until after they have lodged. The median time to correction was 17 days.

This document specifies the business requirements for a guided, mobile-first activity statement lodgment journey that calculates and shows the net amount before the lodger commits. It is to be delivered first as a click-through prototype for stakeholder sign-off, ahead of build.

2. In scope

  • Quarterly BAS, single reporting entity, self-lodger
  • GST labels G1, 1A, 1B
  • PAYG labels W1, W2 and PAYG instalment T7
  • Net amount calculation and display (7A / 7B)
  • Capture, review, declaration and confirmation of one statement

3. Out of scope

  • Authentication and identity proofing
  • Registered tax and BAS agent lodgment channels
  • Payment, payment plans and account balance
  • Amendments and revisions to a lodged statement
  • Fuel tax credits, WET, LCT and instalment notices

4. Business requirements

RefRequirementPriority
REQ-001 The journey shall open with an introduction screen naming the service, stating the reporting period and due date being lodged, and stating its three headline benefits: figures are checked as they are entered, the net amount is shown before the statement is lodged, and a receipt number is issued immediately. Must
REQ-002 The lodger shall be able to start the statement in a single action from the introduction screen, shall be able to see how far through the statement they are at every step, and shall be able to return to a previous step without losing entered data. Must
REQ-003 The statement shall capture the reporting entity’s ABN, the name of the authorised contact making the declaration, and a contact email address for the receipt. Must
REQ-004 The statement shall capture the GST labels for the period: G1 total sales including GST, 1A GST on sales, and 1B GST on purchases. Each label shall be displayed with its label code and its plain-English name. Must
REQ-005 The statement shall capture the PAYG labels for the period: W1 total salary, wages and other payments, W2 amounts withheld from those payments, and T7 the PAYG instalment amount for the quarter. Must
REQ-006 The service shall calculate the net amount for the period and display it before lodgment, showing the arithmetic used and stating in plain English whether the amount is payable to the ATO (label 7A) or refundable to the entity (label 7B). Must
REQ-007 The lodger shall be prevented from progressing while any mandatory field is missing or invalid, and shall be shown which field is at fault, why it was refused, and which business rule was applied. Must
REQ-008 Before lodging, the lodger shall be shown a review screen displaying every label value they entered and the calculated net amount; they shall confirm the declaration; and on lodgment they shall be shown a confirmation screen containing the receipt number and the date and time of lodgment. Must

5. Business rules

RefRule
BR-01ABN is mandatory and must be eleven digits that pass the standard ABN check-digit algorithm. Spaces entered by the lodger are ignored.
BR-02Authorised contact name is mandatory and must be at least two characters.
BR-03Contact email is mandatory and must be a structurally valid address.
BR-04Every monetary label is mandatory, must be zero or greater, and must be a whole dollar amount. Cents are not accepted, and a label value may not exceed twelve digits. Dollar signs and thousands separators entered by the lodger are ignored.
BR-05GST on sales (1A) cannot exceed total sales including GST (G1).
BR-06Amounts withheld (W2) cannot exceed total salary, wages and other payments (W1).
BR-07The net amount is (1A − 1B) + W2 + T7. Where the result is greater than zero it is reported at 7A as an amount payable to the ATO; where it is less than zero it is reported at 7B as an amount refundable to the entity; where it is exactly zero the statement is a nil result and neither label is reported.
BR-08The statement cannot be lodged until the declaration in REQ-008 is confirmed.
BR-09No statement data is transmitted, stored or lodged. This is a prototype for sign-off only.

6. Brand and visual direction

The journey shall use the existing Lodge Assist service design system without modification — the same colour styles, type scale and spacing already in use across the program. Target frame is 390 × 844. The primary user is a sole trader or micro-business owner completing the statement on their own phone, often outside business hours and frequently under time pressure near the due date. Interactive targets must be at least 44 × 44 CSS pixels, and the interface must meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA — in particular, no error may be communicated by colour alone.

7. Acceptance criteria

  • A stakeholder can complete a quarterly statement end to end on a phone-sized screen without assistance.
  • Every screen traces to at least one requirement reference above, and the trace is visible in the running prototype.
  • Invalid input is refused with a visible, specific message naming the rule applied (REQ-007, BR-01 to BR-06).
  • The review screen shows the values the tester actually entered, not sample data (REQ-008).
  • The net amount shown matches an independent hand calculation of BR-07 for at least one payable, one refundable and one nil case.

8. Test and assurance

A test pack shall be produced from this document before build sign-off. Every requirement and every business rule above shall have at least one test case traced to it, and every test case shall name the reference it verifies. Coverage in both directions — requirement to test, and test to requirement — shall be reportable on demand. Negative and boundary cases are required for every rule expressed as a limit or a comparison (BR-01, BR-04, BR-05, BR-06, BR-07).

Note for delivery: this is a sign-off prototype, not a production build. No backend, no identity proofing, no data capture and no integration with the lodgment or accounting systems. It exists so the business can agree the journey before development is scheduled.
